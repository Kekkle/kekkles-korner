title: "My Hyrox Solo: 5 Things I Did Not Expect"
date: 2026-10-13
---

Although I had read a lot of Hyrox tips and tricks and “5 things to never/always do” in the months leading up to my race, there were still some things on race day that I did not expect–and not all were negative.

### I did not expect

1. **The mental fatigue of running 2.5 laps after each station.** My brain had practiced distance, but not redundancy. The mental demotivation of seeing that I had to pass the “IN” one more time took its toll on me. I discovered that training distance on a treadmill or on a long route outdoors, is not the same as running the same loop over and over, and I had not mentally prepared for this reality.
2. **How easy it would be to block out all the spectators and just do my thing.**
3. **How fun and doable the sled pull would feel!** This was the only station I never trained so I was curious how it would feel on race day, and once I got going, I found it satisfying and one of the more enjoyable stations.
4. **Getting a stitch during my runs.** I had considered all other possibilities–Morton's neuroma flare up, muscle cramps, even Rhabdo, but not getting a stitch. Thankfully it didn’t derail me, but rather forced me to slow my pace (walking) and to focus on deep breaths which in the end may have helped me to avoid the muscle cramps.
5. **To feel so full of energy afterwards.** I had expected to feel fatigued, achey, famished and be drenched in sweat. But after the finish line, I just felt full of adrenaline and energy. Not even hungry enough for my Snickers treat.

After the high of the race wore off, dissatisfaction with my finish time set in. Looking over my race breakdown on Roxfit, it was clear that both the Roxzone (almost 10 minutes) and my runs were incredibly slow (ca. 7 minute laps). Granted I walked one lap in 4 out of the 8km runs, but still.

In my next post, I’ll cover the five things I would do differently next time, should I race again.
